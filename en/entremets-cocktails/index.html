Entremets & Number Cakes

For your birthdays and celebrations: unique creations combining aesthetics and indulgence.

Our specialities:

Chocolate Saint-Honoré – A great French pastry classic, revisited with passion. On a golden, flaky homemade puff pastry rests a crown of choux buns filled with an intense dark chocolate pastry cream, all crowned by an airy and generous chocolate mousse. A creation that balances crunch, melt-in-the-mouth texture and deep chocolate richness — tradition and indulgence in perfect harmony.
Number Cake – A spectacular cake crafted in the shape of the number of your choice. Elegant, fully personalised and guaranteed to make a lasting impression at any birthday celebration.
La Rose des Sables – A soft almond biscuit with a crunchy homemade hazelnut praline centre, enrobed in a rich and velvety dark chocolate mousse. A perfect balance of softness and intensity.
L'Anaïs – A delicate almond macaron biscuit paired with a light, airy cream and fresh, tangy raspberries. Freshness and refinement in a subtle, indulgent combination.
La Mauritius – A soft almond macaron biscuit, smooth Bourbon vanilla diplomat cream, with a tropical fruit coulis insert (mango, passion fruit, pineapple). A sun-drenched escape in every slice.
Paris-Brest, My Way – Golden, crisp choux pastry filled with a generous diplomat cream blended with homemade toasted hazelnut praline, finished with an airy whipped cream. A classic revisited with pure indulgence.
Classic Mille-Feuille – Golden, flaky homemade puff pastry layered with a silky pastry cream delicately flavoured with local rum. The timeless elegance of traditional French patisserie.
Lemon Tart – A fine, melt-in-the-mouth shortcrust pastry base with a smooth, tangy lemon and lime cream, crowned with a lightly toasted Italian meringue. Zesty, fresh and irresistible.
Fruit Tart – A buttery shortcrust pastry, soft almond cream, Bourbon vanilla mousseline cream, generously topped with a colourful assortment of fresh seasonal fruits. Vibrant, fresh and generous.
Strawberry & Red Fruit Tart – A golden, perfectly crisp shortcrust pastry base, layered with a soft almond cream and a fragrant vanilla mousseline. Crowned with ripe, juicy strawberries and carefully selected red fruits — generous, fresh and elegant in every bite.
Tarte Tatin – Crafted in the purest respect of tradition, this upside-down tart begins with a golden, flaky homemade puff pastry, generously topped with a carefully selected blend of apples — meltingly soft Golden and slightly tart Granny Smith — caramelised in an irresistibly indulgent salted butter caramel, lifted with a delicate touch of cinnamon. Warmth, depth and authenticity in every bite.
